How to play  ?

A clear path is the only way out.

Tap any part of an arrow. Its head travels straight in the direction it points, and its body follows around the bends.

Check the entire line from the head to the edge. Another arrow anywhere on that line blocks the move. A blocked arrow comes back and costs one mistake.

Clear every arrow before making nine mistakes. There is no timer, and any legal move is safe to make.

Move around: drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, F to fit. H highlights a legal move; Enter plays it. Z undoes a move or mistake. R replays this puzzle.

Every puzzle uses every dot exactly once and has a checked solution. The same grid size and seed reproduce the same board. Works offline.
